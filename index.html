Hi. My craft is programming and everything connected to computers. I pour a lot of effort into it, try my hardest even when it gets tough. It's mine, it's native to me.

But I'm human, and emotions take their toll. When something doesn't work out — be it code, a task, or just some little thing — I start feeling sad. I can go quiet for a long time, retreat into myself, stop noticing what's around me. And sometimes, instead of sadness, anger comes — at myself, at the situation, at the fact that my hands aren't growing from the right place. Then I can snap, say something harsh, even though inside I'm still soft — it's just that emotions take over.

And my mood is like mountain weather. Some days I'm full of energy, cold and collected, telling the truth straight to people's faces and getting things done. And other days, apathy hits me, and I just drop out of reality. I want to see no one, stay silent, stare at one point, and wait until it passes. It doesn't last long. I just need to wait it out, to reboot.

That's how I am. With all my ups and downs, weaknesses, and stubbornness. I don't like talking about myself, but if I'm saying this — it means I trust you and want you to understand me as I am.
